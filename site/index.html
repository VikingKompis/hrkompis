What we do

Where

Six markets: the United Kingdom, Ireland, Denmark, Germany, the Netherlands and Sweden.

Public and private sector, in-house and agency, from HR assistant to Chief People Officer.

When

Every day at 05:00 Stockholm time, before your first coffee.

New ads appear the morning after they are published. Ads that have closed or been taken down drop off.

How

HR roles only, sorted by country, sector, ad language, HR job family and level.

Every listing links to the original ad, so you apply directly with the employer or agency.

Sorted by the work, not the job title

The same job goes by a dozen titles across six languages. A Personalreferent, a P&O-adviseur and an HR Business Partner often do the same work. We sort every role into one of seven HR job families so you see all of it.

  • HR leadershipHR Director, Head of People, CHRO, HR-chef
  • Business partnering & generalistHRBP, HR Advisor, People Partner, HR-konsult
  • Talent acquisitionRecruiter, TA Partner, Sourcer, Employer brand
  • Talent, learning & ODL&D, Leadership development, Talent management
  • Reward & payrollCompensation & benefits, Payroll, Lönespecialist
  • Employee relations & work environmentER, Labour law, Arbetsmiljö, Works councils
  • HR operations & systemsHR admin, HRIS, People operations, Shared services

Where the jobs come from

  • Arbetsförmedlingen (Sweden)
  • Jobnet (Denmark)
  • EURES, the EU job mobility portal (Germany, the Netherlands, Ireland)
  • NHS Jobs (UK)
  • The UK government's Work Hub (UK)
  • publicjobs.ie (Ireland)
  • Career sites of 200+ employers, including Amazon, Unilever, Salesforce, Pfizer, Barclays, Volvo and Spotify

HRKompis is free to use, with no sign-up. We don't ask for your CV and we don't post jobs ourselves.
